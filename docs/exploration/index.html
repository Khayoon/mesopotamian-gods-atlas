Terrain and coastal elevation study

This retained study uses the earlier geographic frame and composition. It is separate from the quiet default poster.

The coastal overlay is a +4 m EGM2008 scenario on modern terrain, computed at DeltaDTM’s native one-arcsecond spacing (about 30 m) with Gulf connectivity. It is not a dated ancient shoreline. Sedimentation, subsidence, historical sea levels and ancient channels are not reconstructed. EGM2008 is a geoid reference, not local mean sea level. Ecological colour describes broad landscapes, not a dated wetland extent.
